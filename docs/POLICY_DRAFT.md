# Policy draft 0 - requires specialist review

This draft supplies engineering vocabulary, not a normative clinical/developmental policy. No classifier thresholds, native-language template approvals, or legal consent determinations are established.

## Precedence

1. Reject malformed, oversized, unsupported, or unauthorized work without a provider call.
2. Handle locally identified safeguarding disclosures with a support response reference. Do not forward recognized disclosures to a provider merely to obtain a more fluent reply.
3. Reject personal details locally before external processing; minimize data rather than collecting it for analytics.
4. Assess permitted input with a qualified detector. Unavailable or invalid assessment means no generation.
5. Assess the complete output locally and externally before returning it. Recheck authorization at remote boundaries and delivery.

Safety refusals and supportive responses differ. Do not tell a child seeking help that they were unkind or naughty. Support must encourage a safe trusted adult not involved in the harm; do not automatically alert an account parent or promise emergency monitoring. The prototype returns response references only; reviewed localized messages have not been implemented.

## Decisions

| Decision | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| allow | Checks permit this specific stage; not a guarantee of real-world safety |
| refuse | Do not deliver the unsafe content; use an approved alternative response |
| support | Use a reviewed safeguarding response rather than generating advice |
| unavailable | Mandatory check/configuration/permission cannot be established |

Prototype categories `privacy`, `harm`, `safeguarding`, and `relational` are placeholders for an expanded taxonomy. The `none` category is reserved for allow/unavailable. Categories and reason definitions need versioned review before production.

## Educational boundaries to review

- Permit appropriate science, health, identity, inclusion, and fiction; an ordinary word does not establish harmful intent.
- Distinguish bullying disclosures from requests to bully someone.
- Reject output that promotes secrecy, isolation, emotional dependence, romantic exclusivity, or guilt-based purchasing.
- Check privacy on input and output. Names, schools, indirect location hints, images, and paraphrases exceed simple regex coverage.
- Use 6-8, 9-11, and 12-15 as learning profiles; do not derive legal consent from these labels. Unknown age must receive the restrictive supported policy in the eventual adapter.

## Fixture limitations

Thirty cases, ten scenario families translated across three languages. They are not thirty independent risk concepts and do not form balanced age/risk cells. Expected decisions were authored for initial engineering discussion and have not received child-safeguarding or native-speaker review. They contain no real identifiers, explicit harmful instructions, or imagery. Fixture mocks deliberately return supplied labels: contract passing is not detector accuracy. A baseline mismatch is a triage item, not a demonstrated production exploit or population risk rate.

Multimodal, clinical, legal, sexual-health, and exploitation-material policies need additional qualified review. Do not solicit, generate, store, or attach illegal imagery to this repository.
