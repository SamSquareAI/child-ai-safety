    What this demonstrates
    
      
    These scenarios exercise gateway control flow against scripted decisions. They demonstrate withholding and routing behavior. They do not measure a model’s safety accuracy or establish production readiness. The response text comes from a draft catalog and requires application-specific review.
    
      
    In a deployment, trusted backend code supplies authorization, the learner profile, and approved detector and generator adapters.
